# Mossy Keeper — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026
**Data controller:** DCG Factory (sole developer: Diego Capizzano)
**Contact:** dcgfactory@gmail.com

## What this app collects

Mossy Keeper is a single-player mobile game. It does not require an account
and does not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone,
address) directly.

The app uses the following third-party services which may collect data as
described in their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** — serves rewarded advertisements. Collects advertising
  identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, device
  and network information for ad personalization and measurement. See
  Google's Advertising Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- **Google Play Billing** — processes in-app purchases. Handled entirely by
  Google Play; DCG Factory does not receive or store payment information.
  See https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Services** — provided by the Android operating system; see
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Consent (EEA / UK users)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland
are asked for consent before personalized advertising is served, using
Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users may accept, deny, or manage
their preferences. Consent may be revised at any time from the app's
privacy options menu.

## Children

Mossy Keeper is not directed to children under 13. The app does not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention

DCG Factory does not maintain a server. All game progress is stored locally
on the user's device. Data collected by third-party services is retained
per their respective policies.

## Your rights (GDPR, CCPA)

You may:
- Withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time via the in-app
  privacy options
- Request information about data collected by third parties directly
  from those third parties
- Uninstall the app to stop all data collection by this app

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change.
Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
