# Mossy Keeper — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026
**Data controller:** DCG Factory (sole developer: Diego Capizzano)
**Contact:** dieguz80@gmail.com

## What this app collects

Mossy Keeper is a single-player mobile game. It does not require an account
and DCG Factory does not directly collect personally identifiable information
(name, email, phone, address).

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as
described in their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** — serves rewarded advertisements. Collects advertising
  identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, and
  device and network information for ad personalization and measurement. See
  Google's Advertising Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- **Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)** — collects
  pseudonymous app-usage and event data (for example: sessions, in-app
  actions, purchase and advertising events, and an app-instance identifier)
  to help us understand engagement, retention, and how the game is played so
  we can improve it. Analytics data is not used to identify you personally.
  See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — collects crash and stability diagnostics
  (stack traces, device model, operating-system version, app version, and
  anonymous installation identifiers) so we can detect and fix crashes. No
  personally identifiable information is collected for this purpose. See
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Google Play Billing** — processes in-app purchases. Handled entirely by
  Google Play; DCG Factory does not receive or store payment information.
  See https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Services** — provided by the Android operating system. See
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland users)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are
asked for consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before
consent-dependent data collection takes place. This consent covers both
personalized advertising (Google AdMob) and analytics data collection
(Firebase Analytics).

Until consent is granted, analytics collection is disabled and personalized
advertising is not served. Users may accept, deny, or manage their
preferences, and consent may be revised at any time from the app's privacy
options menu.

Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) are collected to keep the app stable
and secure; this collection does not include personalized profiling.

## Children

Mossy Keeper is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Data retention

DCG Factory does not maintain its own server. All game progress is stored
locally on the user's device. Data collected by the third-party services
listed above is retained according to their respective policies.

## Your rights (GDPR, CCPA)

You may:

- Withdraw or change consent for personalized ads and analytics at any time
  via the in-app privacy options
- Request information about data collected by the third-party services
  directly from those services
- Uninstall the app to stop all further data collection by this app

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change.
Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
